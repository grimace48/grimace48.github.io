Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Netsy does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. Netsy does not require an account, login, or internet connection to use.

Data Storage

All game data you enter — including team names, player names, shot locations, and stats — is stored locally on your own device for the current session only. This data is not sent anywhere and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Netsy does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Netsy does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because Netsy does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as new features are added to Netsy. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app developer.
